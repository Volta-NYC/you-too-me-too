# Design direction

## Design read

A visual, bilingual local-fashion site for women shopping for everyday pieces, event looks, bridal, and accessories. It uses a polished Seoul and Tokyo editorial language without sanding away the crowded, personal energy of the real boutique.

## Tokens

- Gallery white: `#f7f7f5`
- Ink: `#16151a`
- Storefront raspberry: `#d82a78`
- Deep raspberry: `#ad1659`
- Powder pink: `#f3d5e2`
- Chrome gray: `#cdd0d5`
- Display: Bodoni Moda, used for fashion-editorial moments
- Body and controls: Manrope
- Chinese labels: Noto Sans SC

## Layout and signature

The hero is an asymmetric lookbook spread instead of a generic centered banner. The main recurring signature is the styling rail: garment, bridal, and accessory photography presented as one continuous wardrobe. Editorial scale and sharp image crops are balanced by restrained micro-interactions and one moving category ticker.

Buttons are pill-shaped controls. Photographs and layout panels stay sharp-edged to preserve a magazine-spread feel. Motion is limited to the ticker, image hover scale, navigation underline, and in-view reveals, all disabled or flattened when reduced motion is requested.
