# You Too Me Too research dossier

Research completed August 30, 2026. This file separates verified facts from listing noise and records every image used in the first draft.

## High-confidence business facts

- Business name: You Too Me Too Styling Boutique, also listed as `日韩礼服造型馆` and `日韩限量礼服造型馆`.
- Owner: Grace. The Asian American Federation identifies Grace as the small business owner and describes her work styling customers in outfits that bring out their confidence.
- Ownership attributes: the current Google Business Profile identifies the boutique as women-owned and Asian-owned.
- Address used on the website: 41-22 Main St, Flushing, NY 11355.
- Phone: (646) 617-1888.
- Google rating observed during research: 5.0 from 8 reviews.
- Product categories supported across the storefront sign and current public listings: women’s clothing, wedding and formal dresses, shoes, hats, bags, jewelry, accessories, beauty products, and custom services.
- Social destination: the Google Business Profile links to the boutique’s Xiaohongshu profile.

## Owner story

The [Asian American Federation Facebook feature](https://www.facebook.com/AsianAmericanFederation/videos/698518905920502/) says Grace rebuilt in a new location after a devastating fire at her prior storefront. AAF’s bilingual economic empowerment team helped her establish a stronger Google Maps presence with a refreshed description, targeted keywords, and new photographs. The public post is dated May 14, 2025.

## Source reconciliation

- [Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/xDGCZ7VVGFEp1mXG9) is treated as the primary source for current address, phone, hours, rating, reviews, ownership attributes, and Xiaohongshu link.
- The AAF Facebook caption says `41-23 Main St`, but Google Maps, Chamber of Commerce, Magicpin, the storefront address plate, and the storefront sign point to `41-22 Main St`. The draft uses 41-22.
- [Magicpin](https://magicpin.com/usa/Queens/Flushing/Fashion/You-Too-Me-Too-Styling-Boutique-%E6%97%A5%E9%9F%A9%E7%A4%BC%E6%9C%8D%E9%80%A0%E5%9E%8B%E9%A6%86-%28%E5%A5%B3%E5%A3%AB%E6%9C%8D%E8%A3%85,-%E5%8C%85,-%E9%9E%8B,-%E5%A9%9A%E7%BA%B1,-%E5%8C%96%E5%A6%86%E5%93%81%EF%BC%89/store/24b2850/photos) displays 5.0 from 37 online ratings, but that aggregation cannot be reconciled with Google’s current 8-review count. The website uses the current Google count.
- Magicpin exposes a WhatsApp enquiry link with a non-US number unrelated to the confirmed boutique phone. It is excluded.
- [Chamber of Commerce](https://www.chamberofcommerce.com/business-directory/new-york/flushing/women-s-clothing-store/2026288672-you-too-me-too-ri-han-xian-liang-li-fu-zao-xing-guan) shows only 3 reviews and includes a one-star hair-salon complaint. The boutique is located above a salon, so that review likely concerns the neighboring salon rather than Grace’s business. It is excluded from boutique copy.
- [LocalityBiz](https://localitybiz.com/29237775/you-too-me-too-%E6%97%A5%E9%9F%A9%E9%99%90%E9%87%8F%E7%A4%BC%E6%9C%8D%E9%80%A0%E5%9E%8B%E9%A6%86-queens) mirrors the 5.0 Google review set but is secondary and was used only as a cross-check.
